# Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how the OxRSE Training website (Gutenberg) collects and uses personal data.
The website is run by the [Oxford Research Software Engineering group](https://www.rse.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford.

## Who is responsible for your data

The University of Oxford is responsible for the personal data collected through this website, which makes it the "data controller" under data protection law.
We only use your data to run this website, as described below.

## What data we collect

You can browse the course material without signing in.
When signed in, we collect only the data listed below.

You sign in using a third-party account, such as GitHub or Oxford Single Sign-On.
When you do, we receive and store directly from the sign in provider a subset of the following information from your account:

- your name
- your email address
- your profile picture (a link to the image held by the sign-in provider)
- an identifier for your account with the sign-in provider, and the tokens it issues to confirm your identity

### When you use the website

When you are signed in, we store the information you choose to give us, including:

- events you request to join, and whether you are a student or instructor on them
- courses you enrol on, and when you start and complete them
- your progress on exercises, including any solutions, notes and difficulty ratings you submit
- comments you post on course material, which are visible to other participants and instructors on the same event

### Search

Search is available to signed-in users.
When you search, the text you type is sent from our server to [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), which turns it into a form we can compare with the course material.
The text is not linked to your account, and it is neither stored by this website nor retained by OpenAI.

### Analytics

We use a self-hosted instance of [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/data-policy) to count page visits and understand which material is useful.
Our analytics does not use cookies and does not collect personal, or otherwise identifiable data about your site usage including your IP address.
It does not track you across websites.

## Cookies and browser storage

This website only uses cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary for it to work.
We do not use any form of advertising or tracking cookies, and have no additional optional cookies.

| Name                      | Type            | Purpose                                                        |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `next-auth.session-token` | Cookie          | Keeps you signed in                                            |
| `next-auth.csrf-token`    | Cookie          | Protects sign-in forms from cross-site request forgery         |
| `next-auth.callback-url`  | Cookie          | Returns you to the page you were on after signing in           |
| `activeEvent`             | Local storage   | Remembers which event you are currently working on             |
| `theme`                   | Local storage   | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme            |
| `scrollPosition_*`        | Local storage   | Remembers your scroll position in the sidebar                  |
| Sidebar state             | Session storage | Remembers whether the sidebar is open, until you close the tab |

On secure connections, the cookie names may start with `__Secure-` or `__Host-`.

## How we use your data

We use your data to:

- sign you in and keep your account secure
- manage your participation in events and courses
- save your progress so you can return to it
- let instructors support you during events
- improve the website and course material

## Lawful basis

We use your personal data to give you access to the training material on this website.
Teaching is one of the University's core functions, which data protection law treats as a "task carried out in the public interest" ([Article 6(1)(e) of the UK GDPR](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/public-task/)).

## Who we share your data with

Your data is accessible to authorised members of the Oxford Research Software Engineering group who run this website.
Instructors on an Event can see a list of participants and their progress, and can read comments posted on the event's material.
Your name, profile picture are attached to comments which are visible to other participants and instructors on events you join.

We use the following service providers to run this website:

- [Fly.io](https://fly.io/legal/privacy-policy), which hosts the website and its database in its London region
- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), which processes the text of searches
- your chosen sign-in provider, such as [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) or the University of Oxford

While some of these providers may be based abroad, your data is never transferred outside of the United Kingdom.

We do not sell your data or otherwise share it with any other third party for any purpose.

## Data Retention

We keep your account and the data linked to it for as long as your account is active.
If you would like your account and data deleted, contact us using the details below.

## Your rights

Under data protection law, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you
- ask us to correct inaccurate data
- ask us to delete your data
- ask us to restrict or object to our use of your data
- ask for your data in a portable format

For more information about your rights, and how to exercise them, see the University's [data protection pages](https://compliance.admin.ox.ac.uk/individual-rights).
